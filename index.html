Ary Cahyo Wibowo

I studied computing at STMIK Akakom and now work as a software developer. Some of my earliest projects were simple things I could test at home, like using an ESP8266 and a servo to turn a lamp switch.

Working on my Nissan B13 gave me a different kind of debugging. A no-start meant checking power, ground, and sensor signals before I could narrow it down. Over time, that work grew into a dedicated wiring harness and a Speeduino conversion.
