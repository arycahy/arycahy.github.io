Nissan B13 · Speeduino

A standalone ECU conversion with a modular wiring harness and a custom crank trigger wheel.

Overview

I converted my GA16DE-powered Nissan B13 to a Speeduino standalone ECU to learn how engine management, trigger signals, and vehicle wiring work together.

What I Built

I made a dedicated modular harness, adapted the ECU connection to a 33-pin socket, and fabricated an 18-1 crank trigger wheel mounted on the crank pulley. The setup uses a single cam signal and coil-on-plug ignition.

How I Tested It

I traced intermittent loss of tuning data around radiator-fan shutdown to relay kickback and added flyback diodes. For a separate sync issue, I compared steady engine speeds with sweeps through roughly 1,800 RPM.

Current Status

The engine runs on Speeduino with the revised harness. I am investigating intermittent sync loss during RPM changes.
